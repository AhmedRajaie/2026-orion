01 — MISSION

What This Track Is For

This is not a diploma, and it is not a promise that automation makes trading easy. It is an open-source introduction for people who already understand basic stocks and want to learn how trading ideas become systems.

The Promise
Unlock the stock market as a playground for Egyptian and MENA students, builders, and curious investors.

You do not need to be a quant. You do not need AI. You do not need advanced math. You need to understand rules, testing, risk, and execution clearly enough to experiment responsibly.

The Foundation Track teaches the stock market itself. This Algo Track starts after that: once you know what a stock is, what a price is, and how people trade, the next question is simple — can a trading idea be written as rules?

If it can, it can be tested. If it can be tested, it can be improved. If it can be improved, it can become a small system. That is the mental shift this track is built around.

02 — PRINCIPLES

The Rules of the Track

The goal is to make algorithmic trading accessible without turning it into hype.

No Magic
An algo is just a defined set of rules. The computer adds speed, consistency, and coverage — not supernatural prediction.
No Hype
Automated does not mean profitable. Every part of this track keeps the limits visible.
Risk First
A strategy is incomplete without position sizing, stop rules, drawdown limits, and a kill switch.
Builder Mindset
Articles explain the concepts. Repositories will eventually hold the working examples, templates, and experiments.
↳ The goal is not to make beginners reckless. The goal is to make experimentation safer, clearer, and more honest.
03 — TRACK MAP

Seven Parts, One Clear Path

The track moves from simple rules to live-system thinking. Each part should be short, finite, and understandable without needing to code first.

  • Part 01
    What Is an Algorithm, Really?
    Strip the mystique. An algo is a defined set of rules applied consistently. If a trading idea can be written clearly, it is already algo-able.
    Open
  • Part 02
    Why Algos at All?
    The honest case and the honest limits: emotion, consistency, scale, full-market coverage, overfitting, changing regimes, and why automation is not the same as profit.
    Open
  • Part 03
    Anatomy of a Trade and a Strategy
    Every trade needs entry, take-profit, stop-loss, and duration. Every strategy needs signal, entry rule, exit rule, and position sizing.
    Open
  • Part 04
    Backtesting
    How to test ideas on the past — and why the past lies. Look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, overfitting, commission, slippage, and untouched recent data.
    Open
  • Part 05
    From Backtest to Live
    The gap between paper and real money: order types, broker errors, bad fills, max daily loss, drawdown shutdowns, kill switches, regime changes, and alpha decay.
    Open
  • Part 06
    Architect Your Algo Like a Mini Company
    A serious system is not one giant script. It has roles: data, research, execution, portfolio management, and a risk officer that can shut everything down.
    Open
  • Part 07
    What AI Brings to Algo Trading
    AI as an extension, not a replacement: sentiment, unstructured data, adaptive strategies, retraining, and the danger of overfitting systems that keep changing.
    Open
