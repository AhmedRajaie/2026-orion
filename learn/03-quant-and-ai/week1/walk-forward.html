Walk-Forward & Honest Testing

Any strategy can look brilliant on the past. The only test that counts is the future it never saw.

01

The easiest way to fool yourself

Tune a strategy on historical data long enough and it will look amazing — because you've quietly fitted it to exactly what already happened. That's not skill, it's hindsight. The market won't repeat that history, so the strategy falls apart live. This is the single most common, most expensive mistake in the field.

02

In-sample vs out-of-sample

In-sample (train)
The data you built and tuned the strategy on. Good results here prove almost nothing.
Out-of-sample (test)
A later stretch the strategy never touched during building. Good results here are the ones you can believe.
03

Walk it forward

The honest method: train on a window, test on the next unseen window, then roll both forward and repeat. It mimics real life — you only ever have the past to learn from and the future to be judged on.

The discipline

Never let a model see the test period while learning. In this program the split is built into every model and the RL agent — the test curve is the only score that matters.

Take this with you

Results on data you tuned on are worthless. Trust only performance on periods the strategy never saw.
